Compare the patterns

Look at the structure

Usamos los mismos tres tipos de frase para comparar: affirmative, negative and interrogative.

Type
Present Simple
Present Continuous
Affirmative
Subj + verb + complements

I run every day.
She runs everyday.

Subj + am / are / is + verb (ing) + complements

I am working at this school.
She is working at this school.

Negative
Subj + don’t / doesn’t + verb + complements

I don’t live in Málaga.
She doesn’t live in Málaga.

Subj + am / are / is + not + verb (ing) + complements

I am not running right now.
She isn’t running right now.

Interrogative
Do / Does + subj + verb + complements?

Do you work today?
Does she work today?

Am / Are / Is + subj + verb (ing) + complements?

Are you reading the book?
Is she reading the book?

Present Simple

Short answers

Yes, ___ do / does.   No, ___ don’t / doesn’t.
